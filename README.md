# 📊 Amazon Sales Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an **Amazon Sales Analysis Dashboard** designed to analyze sales performance, product trends, and customer reviews. The dashboard provides an interactive and easy-to-understand view of key business metrics.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze overall sales performance
* Track **YTD and QTD sales**
* Monitor products sold and customer reviews
* Identify monthly and weekly sales trends
* Compare sales performance across product categories
* Identify top-performing products by sales and reviews

## 📈 Dashboard Features

* **YTD Sales:** $2.18M
* **QTD Sales:** $811.09K
* **YTD Products Sold:** 27.75K
* **YTD Total Reviews:** 19.42M
* Monthly Sales Trend
* Weekly Sales Analysis
* Sales by Product Category
* Top 5 Products by YTD Sales
* Top 5 Products by YTD Reviews
* Interactive filters for **Product Category, Quarter, and Year**

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Power BI**
* Data Cleaning & Transformation
* Data Modeling
* DAX
* Data Visualization

## 📊 Key Insights

The dashboard helps users quickly understand:

* Which product categories contribute most to sales
* How sales change throughout the year
* Weekly sales patterns
* Products generating the highest sales
* Products receiving the highest number of reviews
